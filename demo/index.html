How does it work?

3 steps. Zero friction. Total Twitch chat synergy.

1 — Streamer connects & opens lobby

Login with your Twitch account. Pick Boss Raid or Speed Quiz. A Twitch-linked QR code and short URL are instantly ready.

2 — Viewers scan & join via Twitch

Viewers scan on their phone or click the link in Twitch chat. 1-click Twitch connect syncs their avatar and subscriber badge.

3 — Chat attacks the boss live

Every tap deals damage. Scores, podiums, and boss rage mode stream live directly on the transparent OBS overlay.
